Historical Incidents Log:
- Incident INC-492: High utilization on Branch3 uplink was caused by a backup synchronization job (sustained bulk transfer) starting during business hours. Latency spiked to 180ms causing VoIP call drops. The resolution was applying rate-limiting to the bulk data class.
- Incident INC-511: Packet loss on primary link was caused by transient hardware issues on the BGP next-hop router. Rerouting traffic to the backup path resolved the issue.
