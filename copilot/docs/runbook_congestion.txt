Runbook: Congestion Mitigation on Branch Links
- In case of congestion on the primary MPLS uplink (utilization > 90% or latency > 150ms):
  1. Shift VoIP and video traffic classes to the backup SD-WAN tunnel (broadband-tunnel-01) to protect real-time services.
  2. Apply a rate-limit (QoS policing) on the bulk-data class (e.g. limit to 10% of total bandwidth) to stop saturation.
  3. Pre-stage reroute of non-critical branch traffic via the secondary path. Confirm BGP next-hop visibility before applying.
- Verification: After applying mitigation, check utilization on the primary path; it should drop back below 70% within 30 seconds.
